# Self-hosted 360° showroom credits and rights

The 360° viewer is implemented in this repository. It does not use third-party embedded viewers or remotely hosted 3D models.

## Local media
No 360° video files have been added yet.

For every future file, record:
- Exact vehicle and model year
- Local filename
- Creator / renderer
- Original source of any 3D asset used
- License or written permission allowing rendering, modification and website use
- Attribution requirements and modifications
- Date rights were checked

Do not add a video unless it shows the exact vehicle and the rights permit its use. No third-party viewer should be reintroduced as a fallback.
