## Self-hosted video files
No video footage has been downloaded, generated, or uploaded to this folder yet.
When a video is added, record:
- Vehicle and exact model year/body style
- Filename
- Creator / camera operator
- Original source URL
- License or written permission explicitly allowing redistribution and website use
- Required attribution and any modifications
- Date rights were checked

A source video must be a true full 360° exterior turntable of the exact car. Do not substitute
driving footage, assembly footage, still photos, generic cars or unrelated model years.
