## Video requirements
- MP4 container, H.264 video, AAC audio optional (muted by default).
- 1920x1080 or 1440x1080, 24–30 fps, 6–15 seconds, seamless loop.
- Full, unobstructed 360° exterior rotation of the exact vehicle/model year.
- Stable camera, consistent lighting, vehicle centered, no titles or watermarks.
- Prefer 8–20 MB per clip for GitHub Pages; optimize with H.264 CRF 22–25.
- Only add footage when redistribution and intended website use are permitted.
- Record creator, original source URL, exact license/permission and attribution in `CREDITS.md`.

## Research note
A search located a public-domain U.S. National Archives film of Model T assembly, but it is
historical factory footage, not a 360° turntable video. It is therefore not suitable as a
substitute. Stock footage found for the GT40 was editorial-only, and the currently referenced
Sketchfab GT40 page discloses third-party sourcing and non-commercial restrictions despite its
displayed CC BY label. The GT40 is held back until rights are clarified.

The Model T, Model A and 1965 Mustang Sketchfab pages display CC BY attribution licenses for
their 3D models. This does **not** grant rights to unrelated video footage or prove that any
video has been created.
