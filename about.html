THE STORY BEHIND THE COLLECTION

More than a timeline.
A world of stories.

Ford Motor Company was founded in 1903. The Model T and moving assembly line helped transform personal transportation and modern manufacturing.

Across the decades, Ford's story expanded from practical family cars to performance icons, pickups and off-road vehicles. Each model reflects the needs, ambitions and design language of its time.

This collection brings together a selection of those memorable vehicles in one place for enthusiasts, learners and curious visitors.
